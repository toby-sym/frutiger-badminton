# Development plan

1. **In progress — cloud court presentation:** the procedural chamfered island, full-sphere gradient sky, pearl sun halo, drifting cloud banks, glossy badminton court, lighting, and net are in place. The court now uses a deeper lagoon palette with a quieter animated sheen, while the camera clear color matches the horizon. Recheck contrast and composition in the artifact.
2. **In progress — character and shuttle presentation:** the generated shuttlecock now uses vertex-colored feather vanes with a two-sided aqua Fresnel sheen, and shot type drives its axial spin. Trails, responsive character/racket animation, procedural contact rings and sparks, generated racket string beds, and distinctive player kit details are in place. Verify shuttle readability and motion in the artifact.
3. **In progress — controller-first local rally:** gamepad movement, aiming, jump, dash, and shot mappings are implemented alongside keyboard and mouse fallback. Movement now accelerates and settles smoothly, and generated character poses react to takeoff, landing, sprinting, and racket follow-through. A compact HUD keeps the serve cue, controls, dash meter, score, and run build readable. Verify controller paths, landing cues, dash feedback, aim preview, and motion in the artifact.
4. **In progress — solo 3v3 and upgrades:** the player and two AI teammates face three generated opponents, with a team-colored reticle marking the assigned returner. Opponents mix clears, drops, and drives toward less-covered lanes, with shot-color trails. Solo runs are first to 7 by two points (cap 9), draft an upgrade every three player points, and offer replay with progression reset. Prism Strings adds faster player shots and a shifting pearl trail; the HUD tracks all six upgrade levels, and the run-end card recaps performance and build choices. Continue balancing the run.
5. Add competitive online 3v3 after the local rally is solid.
6. Continue improving menus, feedback, presentation, and replayability; the title menu now offers Solo Circuit, controls, audio/display settings, and exit with keyboard, mouse, and controller navigation. The score panel labels Aqua and Coral and marks the active or next server. Synthesized cues and an Escape/Start pause overlay are in place. Development CI reuses a successful feature artifact for a matching direct merge to `main` and falls back to a fresh build when the trees differ.

Keep the visual direction procedural: use generated geometry, materials, and shaders without imported 3D models or image textures. Build and inspect each increment before moving on.
