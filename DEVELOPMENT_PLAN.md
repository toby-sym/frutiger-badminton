# Development plan

1. **In progress — cloud court presentation:** the procedural island, full-sphere gradient sky with a generated cloud field, sun halo, cloud banks, badminton court, lighting, and net are in place. A blue lacquer court shader adds fine grain, broad light patches and stylized window reflections; an open glass pavilion with upper galleries, fixtures and floating bubbles frames the view. Check sky, contrast, glass sorting, and composition in the Windows artifact.
2. **In progress — character and shuttle presentation:** the generated shuttlecock uses vertex-colored feather vanes with a two-sided aqua Fresnel sheen, and shot type drives its axial spin. Characters have rounded limbs, shoes attached to the leg pivots, eased body lean, landing compression, a racket swing that returns smoothly to rest, and height-responsive procedural court shadows. Trails, contact effects, racket string beds, and player kit details are in place. Verify characters, shadows and shuttle motion in the artifact.
3. **In progress — controller-first local rally:** gamepad movement, aiming, jump, dash, and shot mappings are implemented alongside keyboard and mouse fallback. Movement accelerates and settles smoothly, and generated character poses react to takeoff, landing, sprinting, and racket follow-through. Airborne serves use a consistent shuttle position relative to the player. A compact HUD keeps the serve cue, controls, dash meter, score, and run build readable. Verify controller paths, airborne serves, landing cues, dash feedback, aim preview, and motion in the artifact.
4. **In progress — solo 3v3 and upgrades:** the player and two AI teammates face three generated opponents, with a team-colored reticle marking the assigned returner. The opposing receiver remains assigned for a whole shot, and the other opponents return to their starting sides. Opponents mix clears, drops, and drives toward less-covered lanes, with shot-color trails. Solo runs are first to 7 by two points (cap 9), draft an upgrade every three player points, and offer replay with progression reset. Prism Strings adds faster player shots and a shifting pearl trail; the HUD tracks all six upgrade levels, and the run-end card recaps performance and build choices. Continue balancing the run.
5. Add competitive online 3v3 after the local rally is solid.
6. Continue improving menus, feedback, presentation, and replayability; the title menu now offers Solo Circuit, controls, audio/display settings, and exit with keyboard, mouse, and controller navigation. The score panel labels Aqua and Coral and marks the active or next server. Synthesized cues and an Escape/Start pause overlay are in place. Development CI reuses a successful feature artifact for a matching direct merge to `main` and falls back to a fresh build when the trees differ.

Keep the visual direction procedural: use generated geometry, materials, and shaders without imported 3D models or image textures. Build and inspect each increment before moving on.
